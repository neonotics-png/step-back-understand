# ADÉLA, *PRIMA* (2026)

*Researched 2026-11-03 from press, label releases and reviews. Sources are listed at the end.*

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Artist | ADÉLA (Adéla Jergová), Slovak singer, songwriter and dancer, born 27 Nov 2003 in Petržalka, Bratislava |
| Album | *PRIMA*, her debut studio album, after the 2025 EP *The Provocateur* |
| Released | 4 September 2026 |
| Label | Capitol Records. Some outlets list Polydor, which is probably the UK/European release, but I couldn't confirm that |
| Executive producers | Dylan Brady (of 100 gecs) and Blake Slatkin. Cashmere Cat produced "I'm The Man" |

## Who she is (the context that matters)

- **Ballet first.** She trained as a ballet dancer and moved to Vienna at 14 and London at 15. During COVID she went home to Bratislava and switched to pop.
- **The KATSEYE "villain."** She was a contestant on HYBE x Geffen's *The Debut: Dream Academy*, the competition that formed the girl group KATSEYE. She was in the first group cut, tying for last after a "Pink Venom" mission. Netflix's 2024 docuseries *Pop Star Academy: KATSEYE* gave her a villain edit, and she got online backlash for complaining about perceived favoritism.
- **Turned the backlash into a launch.** Within weeks she put out "Homewrecked" on her own, followed by "Superscar" in late 2024 and "Machine Girl" (co-produced by Grimes) in early 2025. Capitol signed her in May 2025. *The Provocateur* EP (Aug 2025) included "SexOnTheBeat", whose video has a Christina Aguilera cameo, and "DeathByDevotion". The EP was edgier and more electropop/experimental, and was shortlisted for a Czech Anděl Award.
- In April 2026 she opened for Demi Lovato's North American tour.

## What the album is about

- **The title.** "PRIMA means 'first'" because it's her debut. It also refers to the *prima ballerina*, the company's top dancer: she wants to be "the prima ballerina of pop."
- **Her stated goal:** "I want this album to sound exactly like a 22-year-old girl." The album pairs hard with soft: vulnerability next to swagger and humor, "muscular beats with feminine energy."
- **Recurring themes:** taking control of her own story after the villain edit, pursuing pop stardom, flipping gender roles ("I'm The Man", "Boys"), sex-positivity ("Fantasize", "Hitachi"), and her Eastern European roots meeting Western pop culture ("KGB", "Ain't In LA").
- **Look and style:** campy, "wink-wink, almost ironic" choreography. She and stylist Chris Horan drew on "Slavic Doll" fashion and '90s Eastern European rave style.

## Track by track

1. **KGB**: the opener and a pre-album single. In the video she plays a spy soaking up American pop culture. It ends with a disclaimer that the Soviet imagery is "an acknowledgment of where I'm from," not an endorsement, and that she stands with Ukraine. Critics call it a sardonic "firecracker."
2. **Nicole Kidman**: the breakout hit, released as the focus track with the album. She wrote it after a Golden Globes party where her corset was "so tight… I feel like Nicole Kidman right now." Hannah Lux Davis directed the video, which references *Eyes Wide Shut*, *Moulin Rouge!*, *Birth*, *Babygirl* and Kidman's AMC Theatres ad. It went viral on TikTok, and Madonna and a Chanel lip-sync with Pedro Pascal, Dua Lipa, Kristen Wiig and Kidman herself joined in. Kidman has said she knows who ADÉLA is.
3. **I'm The Man**: produced by Cashmere Cat and plays with gender roles.
4. **Boys**: a club track with glossy late-'90s/early-2000s production. Clash compares it to Missy Elliott and The Neptunes. The Line of Best Fit calls it too close to the songs it borrows from.
5. **Red Bottoms**: a pre-album single and a slow-burn, coy ballad. Her 2026–27 headline tour is named after it.
6. **Hitachi**: sex-positive. Clash calls it one of her strongest vocals, while The Line of Best Fit calls it half-baked.
7. **Fantasize**: a sex-positive affirmation.
8. **Starving Artist**: about the grind of trying to make it. One critic calls it fairly standard commercial pop.
9. **Marijuana**
10. **Therapy**: diary-like. She wrote it while sick on tour, and it's about her bond with her older sister 6,000 miles away. It has hyperpop touches from Dylan Brady.
11. **Ain't In LA**: the closer and third single. It draws on her mother's youth in communist Czechoslovakia, when Western pop posters were banned and her grandfather, a miner in West Germany, smuggled them across the border. Fred Gervais directed the video, which was shot in Bratislava. The main message: "You don't have to leave your country." A key lyric: "I'd rather be a nobody than to be like everyone." Its sound recalls mid-2010s pop and hip-hop, early A$AP Rocky and *Bangerz*-era Miley.

## Commercial performance

- **Billboard 200:** No. 4 debut with 56K units, beating 40–45K forecasts. It's her first top 10 album, and it hit No. 1 on Spotify's US and global Top Albums Debut charts.
- **"Ain't In LA"** was her first Hot 100 entry. It debuted at No. 85 and climbed to No. 61. It also made the UK Top 20.
- **"Nicole Kidman"** overtook it and became her first US top 10, a first for a Slovak artist. Sources disagree on the exact Hot 100 peak: Capital FM says No. 9, Wikipedia says No. 6, and earlier reports gave No. 11 before it climbed. Check billboard.com for the final figure. It peaked at No. 4 in the UK and No. 3 on the Billboard Global 200. Its Hot 100 peak is higher than any KATSEYE single's, and the press played that up heavily.
- **Touring:** her North American headline run (Sept–Oct 2026, including two nights each at Brooklyn and the LA Fonda) sold out. Two Mexico City shows followed, and a sold-out Europe/UK leg runs 11 Jan to 3 Feb 2027, Prague to Warsaw.

## Critical reception (mixed to positive)

| Outlet | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Clash (Robin Murray) | **8/10.** "Palpable star quality", an "unrelenting debut" and a coherent pop statement with hip-hop and neo-industrial touches |
| Rolling Stone | Positive: "Adéla's Risk-Taking Pays Off on *Prima*." Confident, maximalist pop, with "Ain't In LA" singled out |
| Daily Illini | 9/10 |
| Ratings Game Music | B+, "a great debut" |
| The Line of Best Fit (Douglas Jardim) | **6/10.** "Plays dress-up with pop history." Too much nostalgia and borrowing, and safer than *The Provocateur*: "PRIMA pales in comparison" |
| Sputnik Music | 3.0/5, more misses than hits, though "Ain't In LA" is a standout |

**Consensus:** the album is a strong commercial pop launch with real charisma. The bangers ("KGB", "Nicole Kidman", "Ain't In LA") stand out. The main criticism is that it's more polished and less adventurous than her Grimes-adjacent EP, and leans on references to Britney, Gwen, Madonna, Miley and Ariana. Several critics compare it to Addison Rae's 2025 debut, which was in the same lane.

## The short version

*PRIMA* is a 22-year-old ex-ballerina, cast as KATSEYE's "reject" villain, recasting herself as pop's lead dancer. It's a tight 11-track, Capitol-backed pop record executive-produced by Dylan Brady and Blake Slatkin. It mixes swagger and camp with songs about her family history behind the Iron Curtain. It produced her first top-10 hit ("Nicole Kidman") and a No. 4 album debut. Critics liked the star power more than the originality.
